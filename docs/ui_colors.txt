Accent Colors: To add visual interest, consider using an accent color sparingly.
This could be a color that aligns with your brand or a hue that complements gray, like a muted blue, green, or even a soft gold.
For instance:
#4A90E2 (Soft Blue)
#A2A2A2 (Muted Green)
#C0A383 (Soft Gold)


Soft Blue: A calm and professional color, excellent for highlights and buttons.
Example: #4A90E2 or #007BFF.

Muted Green: Great for success messages or confirmations.
Example: #4CAF50 or #8BC34A.

Vibrant Orange: Good for warnings or calls to action.
Example: #FF9800 or #FF5722.

Bright Red: For errors or important alerts.
Example: #F44336 or #D32F2F.

Deep Purple: Can add a touch of sophistication and depth.
Example: #673AB7 or #9C27B0.

Teal: A unique and modern choice that can complement gray nicely.
Example: #009688 or #4DB6AC.

Warm Yellow: For highlighting important elements or notifications.
Example: #FFEB3B or #FFC107.
